Our Story

About
the Academy

After playing and coaching on the professional circuit, and training students who went on to compete at top schools in the nation such as Columbia University, Stanford University, Princeton University, and Johns Hopkins University, we developed a system that creates high-level players.

Our coaching philosophy is very simple: Build your mindset, improve your footwork, develop clean technique, and prioritize fitness and nutrition. When all of these elements come together, players are able to perform at their highest level and reach their full potential.
